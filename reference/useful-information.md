A reference page from the family cookbook — no fixed yield, just kitchen know-how and a menu index.

## Recipes that adapt to a crockpot

- Chicken a la Hindu (reduce water amount by half)
- Hungarian Goulash (reduce water amount by half)
- Pot Roast with Gravy
- Stew with Wine

Put all ingredients into the crockpot, set on low, and cook all day. Potatoes and fresh
vegetables do not cook well in a crockpot, so precook them if you want to use them in a
crockpot recipe.

## Roasting temperatures

- Turkey – 175 degrees
- Chicken – 175 degrees
- Pork – 165 degrees

Beef:

- Rare – 130 degrees
- Medium – 150 degrees
- Well – 170 degrees
