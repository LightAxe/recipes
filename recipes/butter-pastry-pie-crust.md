    unit: tbsp

instructions:
  - "Put the flour into the food processor."
  - "Cut the butter into small pieces and add them and the sugar."
  - "Start processing while adding just enough ice water so that the pastry holds together."
  - "Roll the pastry out on a lightly floured board into a circle large enough to fill the pie plate."
  - "Line the plate and chill."
  - text: "Bake at 400°F (205°C) until desired doneness, about 10–12 minutes."
    timer: PT12M
