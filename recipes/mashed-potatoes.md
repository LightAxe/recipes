title: Mashed Potatoes
description: A flexible, scale-to-the-crowd method for mashed potatoes — boil peeled potato chunks, drain, then mash with butter and your choice of milk, half & half, or cream.
course: side
cuisine: American
tags: [potato]
yield: varies — about one potato per person, plus one extra
ingredients:
  - item: potatoes
    prep: peeled and cut into roughly one-inch pieces
    note: "\"Use one potato per person to serve, plus one extra.\""
  - item: water
    note: enough to cover all the potato pieces
  - qty: 1
    unit: tbsp
    item: salt
    note: for the boiling water
  - item: butter
    note: "\"2–4 tablespoons (or more), depending on amount\""
  - item: milk, half & half, or cream
    note: "\"¼–½ cup (or more)\""
instructions:
  - "Peel the potatoes and cut into pieces, about one square inch. Put into a pot big enough to cover all the potato pieces with water."
  - "Fill the pot with cool water and add about a tablespoon of salt."
  - text: "Cover, place over medium-high heat, and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer about 15–20 minutes, or until a knife or fork can easily pierce through the chunks."
    timer: PT20M
  - "When the potatoes are done, drain thoroughly. Leave the potatoes in the pot and add 2–4 tablespoons of butter and about ¼–½ cup milk, half & half, or cream, depending on how many potatoes you're cooking. Be careful not to add too much liquid."
  - "Mash to desired consistency with an electric mixer or a manual potato masher."
notes:
  - "Add the butter and liquid gradually — be careful not to add too much liquid."
datePublished: 2026-06-29
---

A method page from the family book. The amounts are deliberately loose: figure one
potato per person plus one extra, then mash with as much butter and milk, half & half,
or cream as it takes to reach the consistency you like.
