instructions:
  - "Blend 3–4 eggs with 1 cup (total) of any combination of milk, half and half, cream, and/or cottage cheese. Add salt, pepper, garlic, or other seasonings to taste. Set aside."
  - "Prepare about 1½ cups of filling — cooked meats or diced veggies such as chicken, spinach, onion, broccoli, ham, cabbage, or bell peppers. Sauté and drain vegetables thoroughly to remove as much water as possible."
  - "Place the filling in the unbaked crust. Top with 1–1½ cups grated cheese, any kind."
  - "Pour the egg mixture over all."
  - text: "Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 40–60 minutes, until a knife inserted comes out clean."
    timer: PT50M
notes:
  - "The custard is any combination of milk, half and half, cream, and/or cottage cheese totaling 1 cup — use what you have."
  - "Filling and cheese are open-ended: about 1½ cups of cooked meat or diced vegetables, plus 1–1½ cups of any grated cheese."
