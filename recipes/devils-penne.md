Rob's own weeknight-fancy pasta: chicken breast pounded thin, dredged, and pan-fried, then
folded into penne with a white-wine cream sauce built in the same pan — sun-dried tomatoes,
Parmesan, and wilted spinach. The olives, artichoke hearts, and sliced chicken go in a big
serving bowl and the sauce and penne get poured over the top.
