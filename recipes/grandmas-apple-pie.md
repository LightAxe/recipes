We've made this pie every Thanksgiving for as long as anyone can remember. Grandma Ruth
learned it from *her* mother and never wrote down the amounts — this version was finally
pinned down one afternoon by measuring everything as she went. The lattice top is
optional; she usually just cut three slits and called it done.
