title: Peppermint Ice Cream
description: A festive churned peppermint ice cream tinted pink with red food coloring and studded with crushed peppermint candies — a Christmas favorite.
course: dessert
cuisine: American
tags: [peppermint, ice-cream, christmas, vegetarian]
servings: 8
yield: about 1.5 quarts
ingredients:
  - qty: 1
    unit: pint
    item: whipping cream
    prep: not heavy cream
    grams: 454
    gramsSource: "King Arthur chart (1 cup cream = 227 g)"
  - qty: 1
    unit: pint
    item: whole milk
    grams: 454
    gramsSource: "King Arthur chart (1 cup milk = 227 g)"
  - qty: 0.5
    unit: cup
    item: granulated sugar
    grams: 99
    gramsSource: "King Arthur chart (1 cup = 198 g)"
  - qty: 1
    unit: tsp
    item: peppermint extract
  - qty: 3
    unit: drops
    item: red food coloring
  - qty: 50
    item: peppermint candies
    prep: hard candies, or equivalent candy canes
    grams: 250
    gramsApprox: true
    gramsSource: "typical starlight peppermint ≈ 5 g each"
instructions:
  - text: "Slightly warm 1 cup of the milk in the microwave, about a minute. Stir in the sugar until it dissolves."
    timer: PT1M
  - "Add the second cup of the milk to the mixture and stir in the red food coloring."
  - text: "Pour the cream, milk/sugar mixture, and peppermint extract into the ice cream maker. Turn on the ice cream maker and set the timer for 20 minutes."
    timer: PT20M
  - "Place the unwrapped candy into a bag and break it into small pieces with a hammer."
  - text: "After the ice cream mixture has been processing for 20 minutes, pour in the candy pieces and continue to mix for another 5 minutes."
    timer: PT5M
  - text: "Pour the mixture into a container and freeze for at least 2 1/2 hours."
    timer: PT2H30M
notes:
  - "Use whipping cream, not heavy cream."
  - "The candy amount is flexible — use any peppermint hard candies or the equivalent in candy canes."
datePublished: 2026-06-29
