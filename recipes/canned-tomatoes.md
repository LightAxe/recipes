title: Canned Tomatoes
description: Home-canned whole or halved tomatoes, put up by either raw pack or hot pack with bottled lemon juice and pickling salt. A method recipe for as many jars as your tomatoes fill.
course: canning
cuisine: American
tags: [tomato, vegetarian, vegan]
yield: as many quart or pint jars as your tomatoes fill
ingredients:
  - item: ripe tomatoes
    prep: skinned and cored
  - qty: 2
    unit: tbsp
    item: bottled lemon juice
    note: "per quart jar (1 tablespoon per pint) — required to acidify; use bottled, not fresh, for reliable acidity"
    grams: 30
    gramsApprox: true
    gramsSource: "≈ water density (1 tbsp ≈ 15 ml)"
  - qty: 1
    unit: tsp
    item: pickling salt
    note: "optional, for flavor — per quart jar (1/2 teaspoon per pint)"
    grams: 6
    gramsApprox: true
    gramsSource: "King Arthur — table salt 1 tsp = 6 g"
instructions:
  - text: "Skin and core the tomatoes, leaving them whole or cutting them in half."
    section: Prep
  - text: "Pack tomatoes into jars, pressing gently to fill spaces. Leave 1/2 inch (1.3 cm) headspace."
    section: Raw pack
  - text: "Add 2 tablespoons bottled lemon juice to each quart (1 tablespoon per pint) to acidify, plus 1 teaspoon pickling salt per quart (1/2 teaspoon per pint) for flavor, if desired."
    section: Raw pack
  - text: "Adjust lids and process pints and quarts 85 minutes in a boiling-water bath (at 0–1,000 ft; increase the time at higher altitudes)."
    section: Raw pack
    timer: PT85M
  - text: "In a pot, bring tomatoes to boiling for 10 minutes."
    section: Hot pack
    timer: PT10M
  - text: "Pack the hot tomatoes into jars, leaving 1/2 inch (1.3 cm) headspace."
    section: Hot pack
  - text: "Add 2 tablespoons bottled lemon juice to each quart (1 tablespoon per pint) to acidify, plus 1 teaspoon pickling salt per quart (1/2 teaspoon per pint) for flavor, if desired."
    section: Hot pack
  - text: "Adjust lids and process pints and quarts 85 minutes in a boiling-water bath (at 0–1,000 ft; increase the time at higher altitudes)."
    section: Hot pack
    timer: PT85M
notes:
  - "Raw pack and hot pack are two options for the same jars — choose one. For whole or halved tomatoes packed in their own juice, USDA/NCHFP processing is 85 minutes for both pints and quarts in a boiling-water bath at 0–1,000 ft; increase the time at higher altitudes (see the National Center for Home Food Preservation, nchfp.uga.edu)."
  - "The 85-minute time is the tested USDA/NCHFP process for whole or halved tomatoes packed in their own juice — so keep the tomatoes whole or halved. Crushed or chopped tomatoes have a different tested procedure and processing time; use NCHFP's crushed-tomatoes directions if you want to chop them."
  - "The original family card called for less lemon juice and much shorter processing times; the acidification and times here have been updated to current USDA safe-canning guidance."
  - "For basil flavor, add fresh basil when you open and use the jar — not in the jar before canning. Fresh basil is a low-acid addition that isn't part of the tested tomato process, so keeping it out of the jar keeps the batch on the safe, tested method."
