title: Caramels
description: Old-fashioned homemade caramels cooked with cream, Karo syrup, and evaporated milk to the firm-ball stage, then poured out and cut into squares.
course: dessert
cuisine: American
tags: [caramel, candy]
servings: 64
yield: about 1 large rimmed cookie sheet of caramels
ingredients:
  - qty: 4
    unit: cup
    item: sugar
    grams: 792
    gramsSource: "King Arthur chart (1 cup granulated = 198 g)"
  - qty: 2
    unit: cup
    item: Karo syrup
    grams: 624
    gramsSource: "King Arthur chart (1 cup corn syrup = 312 g)"
  - qty: 1
    item: can evaporated milk
  - qty: 1
    unit: tsp
    item: salt
    grams: 6
    gramsSource: "King Arthur chart (1 tsp table salt = 6 g)"
  - qty: 4
    unit: cup
    item: heavy whipping cream
    grams: 908
    gramsSource: "King Arthur chart (1 cup heavy cream = 227 g)"
  - qty: 2
    unit: tsp
    item: vanilla
    grams: 9
    gramsApprox: true
    gramsSource: "standard (vanilla extract 1 tsp ≈ 4.3 g)"
instructions:
  - "Combine the sugar, Karo, and 2 cups of the cream in a large heavy saucepan. Slowly bring to a boil and stir constantly."
  - "Very slowly drizzle in the remaining cream, keeping the mixture at a boil. Let it reduce a little and then add the canned milk in the same manner."
  - "Continue stirring and cook to firm ball stage."
  - "Remove from heat and add the vanilla. Pour into a buttered large, sided cookie sheet and let cool."
  - "Store in a cool place and keep lightly covered to prevent drying."
notes:
  - "Cook to the firm-ball stage (the candy holds a firm but pliable ball when dropped in cold water)."
