Chiles and two kinds of cheese are layered in a 9×13 dish, then topped with a light
custard made by folding a flour-thickened egg-yolk-and-evaporated-milk mixture into
stiffly beaten egg whites. Add cooked chicken for a heartier main. Baked covered at a low
oven so the custard sets gently around the cheese and chiles.
