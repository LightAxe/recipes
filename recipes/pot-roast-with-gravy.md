instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 325°F (165°C)."
  - "Season the roast all over with garlic salt and pepper."
  - "In a large skillet or pot, thoroughly brown the seasoned roast in the oil, then turn off the heat."
  - "Mix together the cold water, Kitchen Bouquet, and flour until thoroughly combined — easiest done by shaking them in a jar or covered container."
  - "Pour the mixture into the skillet with the meat, turn the heat up to medium-high, and stir until it begins to boil."
  - text: "Cover the skillet and put it into the oven for about 2 to 2½ hours, or until the roast is fork tender."

  - "Serve with mashed potatoes or rice."
