instructions:

  - "Add the chopped onion to the pan and place into a hot oven at 425°F (220°C), uncovered. (The pan can also be placed onto a grill with the lid closed.)"
  - text: "Check the pork in about 45 minutes for doneness (170°F / 75°C). Remove from the pan and allow to rest on a platter."
    timer: PT45M
  - "Add the chicken broth, red wine vinegar, mustard, and lemon juice to the pan. Bring to a boil and reduce by about 1/3. Thicken with the cornstarch."
  - "Pour the sauce over the sliced pork on the platter."
  - "Serve with rice."
