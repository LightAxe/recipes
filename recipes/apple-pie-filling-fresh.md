Printed from myhomecooking.net ("Apple Pie Filling"). Unlike the canned, by-the-quart
version in the family book, this one is mixed fresh and used straight away: you soak the
sliced apples, stir up a quick sugar-flour-cinnamon coating sharpened with a little vinegar
and lemon, and toss the two together. A handwritten note suggests finishing it in a pie
crust with a streusel topping.
