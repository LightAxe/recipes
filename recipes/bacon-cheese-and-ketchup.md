instructions:
  - "Read all the instructions before beginning."
  - "Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C)."
  - "Cook the bacon crispy — you'll need 1½–2 slices per serving. How crispy? Just crispy enough that it breaks apart easily by hand. On a 1–10 scale where 10 is burned, 8 is crumbly in your hands, 4 is chewy, and 1 is probably not safe to eat, take it to about a 7 at this stage — it'll reach an 8 once the finished sandwich goes into the oven. (Was this too much bacon detail? No — bacon is important.)"
  - "Lightly toast the white bread — ideally Wonder Bread or other similarly terrible bread."
  - "While the toast is still warm, lay on a slice of cheddar, then the bacon (broken in half so it fits), and top with ketchup. On the amount, opinions differ — some drizzle it like frosting onto a toaster strudel, some insist on a thin layer. Go with a somewhat generous drizzle, maybe four or five lines. You should be able to taste the ketchup in every bite."
  - text: "Into the oven for 5–10 minutes, until the cheese gets a little melty and the ketchup starts to caramelize. Don't let the ketchup burn."

An unapologetically lowbrow open-faced sandwich, and a house favorite. Crispy bacon
and melty cheddar on toasted cheap white bread, finished with enough ketchup that you
taste it in every bite and baked just until it starts to caramelize.
