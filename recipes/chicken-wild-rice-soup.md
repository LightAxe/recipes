instructions:
  - "Start cooking the rice in a big pot with the chicken broth. Keep the seasoning packet aside — don't throw it out."
  - text: "Melt the butter in a saucepan, add the carrots, celery (and onions, if using), and sauté about 5 minutes."
    timer: PT5M
  - text: "Add the reserved seasoning packet and cook about 5 more minutes, or until the vegetables soften."
    timer: PT5M
  - "In a separate container, mix the flour and pepper."
  - "Slowly add the flour mixture to the vegetables, stirring to make a roux (it'll be like a thick buttery paste), and cook a few more minutes to get rid of the raw flour taste."
  - "Whisk in the cream a little at a time and stir until smooth and thickened. If it's really milky, up the heat a little and keep stirring until it thickens."
  - text: "Add to the rice and cook covered about 15-20 minutes over low-medium heat, keeping it no higher than a low simmer so it doesn't stick to the bottom of the pot, until the rice is all the way done."
    timer: PT20M
notes:
  - "Mary doesn't like onions, so she skips the fresh onion and uses onion powder instead."
  - "Keep the rice seasoning packet — don't throw it out! Near East brand is Mary's go-to, but any quick-cook long grain & wild rice will do."
  - "Dutch oven method: cook the vegetables the same way, then add the broth and deglaze the bottom of the Dutch oven. Bring to a low simmer, then pour in the rice and cook until done. Add the cream last."
