instructions:
  - "In a blender, combine the chocolate chips, sugar, and eggs. Blend."
  - "Add the hot milk and liquor and blend at medium speed until the mixture is smooth."
  - "Pour half of the chocolate mixture into the bottom of the pastry shell and put into the freezer."
  - "Blend the cream cheese, powdered sugar, and vanilla."
  - "Fold a small amount of the whipped cream into the cheese mixture, then fold in the remainder."
  - "When the chocolate mixture in the freezer cools, layer the cheese mixture in the pastry shell."
  - "Top with the remainder of the chocolate mixture."
  - "Chill thoroughly before serving."
