A reference page from the family book covering two everyday pan gravies. It reads as
technique rather than a precise recipe: brown an equal amount of flour in the skimmed
fat, stir the drippings back in, and thin to taste — water for a roast-meat brown gravy,
or half milk and half water (with a bouillon cube if it needs a boost) for fried-chicken
gravy.
