---
title: Bavarian Mints
description: A simple two-step chocolate-mint candy — melted milk chocolate beaten smooth with scalded cream, set in a square pan and cut into mints.
course: dessert
tags: [chocolate, mint, candy, no-bake]
servings: 16
yield: one 8-inch square pan
ingredients:
  - qty: 1.5
    unit: lb
    item: milk chocolate
    prep: melted
    grams: 680
    gramsSource: "1.5 lb = 680 g"
  - qty: 0.75
    unit: cup
    item: cream
    prep: scalded and cooled to 130°F (54°C)
    grams: 170
    gramsSource: "King Arthur chart (1 cup heavy cream = 227 g)"
  - qty: 6
    unit: drop
    item: mint oil
  - qty: 1
    unit: tsp
    item: vanilla
    prep: optional
    grams: 4
    gramsApprox: true
    gramsSource: "King Arthur chart (1 tsp = 4 g)"
instructions:
  - text: "Melt the chocolate in the microwave for 2–4 minutes at 50% power, checking it every minute or half-minute."
    timer: PT4M
  - "Beat the chocolate with a mixer until smooth."
  - "Slowly add the scalded cream that has been cooled to 130°F (54°C) or less and beat until smooth."
  - "Add the mint oil and/or vanilla and pour into an 8x8-inch square pan that has been lined with foil or parchment paper."
  - "Chill, then cut to serve."
notes:
  - "Vanilla is optional; use the mint oil and/or the vanilla to taste."
datePublished: 2026-06-29
---
